# Microsoft Store product features (en-us)

Paste into Partner Center → Store listings → **English (United States)** → **Product features**.

Short summaries of the product's key features. The Store shows them as a bulleted list. Up to 20 app features. Each feature is at most 200 characters. Paste the sentence only; Partner Center adds the bullet.

- Read and send over IMAP, POP3, and SMTP. Several mailboxes stay open. Presets fill hosts for Gmail, Microsoft 365, and Italian PEC operators.
- Unwrap a PEC or REM envelope and read the inner message. The type column marks PEC, a receipt, REM, or a digital signature.
- The delivery column follows operator evidence: accepted, deposited, retrieved, or failed. Italian PEC has no read receipt.
- After you send certified mail, Sent is matched to receipts by Message-Id, daticert.xml, and the subject prefix.
- Preview an Italian e-invoice (FatturaPA) as a readable page. Export selected messages as original .eml files.
- From is the connected mailbox. Send uses that SMTP identity. You do not type a free From address.
- Practice labels stay on this PC. Search covers subject, body, attachment text, and those labels.
- Get Messages stores the whole IMAP folder on this PC, as mail.db plus the original .eml files.
- Optional meaning search runs an on-device model. Vectors stay in mail.db. Mail is not sent to a cloud service.
- Rules can move, delete, flag, and label, including a folder on another account. Import JSON or a legacy rules file.
- A folder store is a directory of .eml files plus its own index. Import an Outlook PST into a store.
- Country packs turn Italy, Europe, France, Germany, Spain, and Switzerland evidence labels on or off.
- Retention moves old mail in a folder to Trash. Trash can be kept forever or deleted after a number of days.
- Mark as spam remembers that message on this PC and applies the hint to every mailbox. Not spam clears it.
- Background sync can start at boot and sync every saved mailbox while nobody is signed in to Windows.
- Easy Migration writes a passphrase-locked bundle of mail, accounts, and secrets, and restores it on another PC.
- Gmail and Microsoft 365 sign in through Identity Hub. Passwords and tokens are kept out of settings.json.
- The interface is English, Italian, French, German, or Spanish. Help opens the log and checks for an update.
